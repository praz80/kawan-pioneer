## App name (English)
Pioneer Buddy

## Tagline
Your Indonesian companion for Pi: learn Pi, protect your wallet, and spot scams before they strike.

## Short description (English)
Pioneer Buddy (Kawan Pioneer) is a Bahasa Indonesia companion app for Pi Network Pioneers. Learn Pi step by step, secure your wallet, check suspicious messages with the Scam Shield, and grow with daily quizzes.

## Full description (English)
Pioneer Buddy — "Kawan Pioneer" in Indonesian — helps Indonesia's millions of Pioneers use Pi safely and confidently.

**Learn Pi (Belajar Pi).** Six short modules in Bahasa Indonesia, each ending in a quiz: Pi mining basics and the security circle; wallet and passphrase safety — the single most important lesson in Pi; KYC step by step; the Mainnet Checklist; reading the Block Explorer; and safe transacting.

**Scam Shield (Perisai Penipuan).** Paste any suspicious message or link and the Shield checks it for the red flags that drain Indonesian Pioneers' wallets: passphrase requests, send-Pi-first schemes, fake "unlock" fees, guaranteed-profit claims, impersonation of the Core Team, pressure tactics, and non-official domains. The verdict and the reasons are explained in plain Indonesian. The check runs entirely on the user's device — pasted text is never transmitted anywhere.

**Daily Quiz (Kuis Harian).** Five fresh questions every day with XP, levels (Pioneer → Penjaga → Ahli → Legenda), and streaks that bring users back daily.

**Pi inside.** Sign-in with Pi Authentication, and an optional one-time "Paket Penjaga" (Guardian Pack) premium unlock paid with Pi through the official User-to-App payment flow.

Indonesia is one of Pi's largest communities, yet almost all Pi education is in English — and Indonesian Pioneers are heavily targeted by localized scams. Pioneer Buddy closes both gaps in the language users actually think in.
