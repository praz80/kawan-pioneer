Scam Shield

The Scam Shield explains common red flags in messages and links. It is a learning aid, not a guarantee: a message that shows no red flags can still be dishonest. Always confirm offers, fees, and announcements through official Pi channels before sending any Pi.

Payments

The Guardian Pack (Paket Penjaga) and Lapak Pionir products are paid in Pi through the official Pi Platform User-to-App payment flow, into the receiving wallet registered for this app. Pi payments are recorded on the blockchain and cannot be reversed by us. If a digital item you paid for fails to unlock or is not delivered as described, contact us through the app's listing page in the Pi Ecosystem directory — where the fault is ours, we will deliver the item or return your Pi from our wallet.

Lapak Pionir is a showcase of digital goods and services priced in Pi. The launch catalogue ("etalase perdana") is run by the app team as example sellers; each product card states exactly what is delivered and its price in Pi. Nothing in Lapak Pionir is an investment product, and no listing promises earnings.
