Privacy Policy — Kawan Pioneer (Pioneer Buddy)

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What we collect

Kawan Pioneer is an educational app for Pi Network users. When you sign in with Pi, we receive your Pi username and a unique user ID from the Pi Platform solely to greet you and process in-app purchases. We do not ask for, receive, or store your wallet passphrase — never enter it anywhere except the official Pi Wallet.

Learning progress

Your lessons, quiz scores, XP, streaks, and premium status are stored locally on your own device (browser local storage). They are not uploaded to our server.

Scam Shield checker

The Scam Shield analysis runs entirely on your device. Text you paste into the checker is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Payments

Premium purchases are processed by the Pi Platform. Our server only confirms payment approval and completion with the Pi Platform API; we do not store payment credentials.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the app's listing page in the Pi Ecosystem directory.
